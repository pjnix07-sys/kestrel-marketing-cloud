# Accessibility review (WCAG 2.2 AA)

**Verdict.** Foundations are strong: skip link, labelled landmarks, sane heading order, visible 2px focus rings everywhere, a real dialog trap with Escape and focus return, reduced-motion handling, roving arrow-key chart and journey nodes, and a clean dark theme (axe: zero colour-contrast failures in dark). The gaps are concentrated in four places: a light-theme muted-text token that sits just under 4.5:1, a Connect drawer that loses its initial focus, decisions (approve, dismiss, generate) that never reach a screen reader and drop focus, and undersized 22px chart markers. Nothing blocks a task for a mouse user; keyboard and screen-reader users hit real friction on the approval and drawer flows.

Method: axe-core 4.8.4 on 6 routes x 1440/390 x light/dark (4.14 does not parse in the bundled Chromium), manual keyboard runs, contrast computed from tokens.css. Assumes default persona, AI not connected. Passed: landmarks, headings, skip link, focus rings, reduced motion, no overflow at 390, drawer trap and Escape.

## Findings, ranked

1. **High. Connect drawer does not take focus on open.** kit.js:731-737 focuses the first control in a rAF, but focus lands on `#connect-create` and is immediately blurred to BODY (observed by focusin/out logging, keyboard and mouse). Cause: `LinkBtn` is a component defined inside the render of `Panel` (connect.js:~98), so any re-render remounts the anchor and drops focus. Result: first Tab goes to the Close button and keyboard users are not announced into the dialog (2.4.3, 4.1.2). Fix: render it as a plain function call (`LinkBtn(...)`) or hoist it to module scope; also pass `initialFocus: '#connect-paste'`. Trap, Escape and focus return to `#app-connect-btn` do work.
2. **High. Approve/dismiss are silent and drop focus.** agent-ui.js:~391-396, 416: after Dismiss > reason, focus falls to BODY inside a modal drawer (verified); after Approve no toast, no live region, card swaps state quietly (4.1.3, 2.4.3). Fix: on decision, move focus to the card's heading (tabIndex -1) or next card, and push a toast or write to one persistent `role="status"` region ("Approved: <title>", "Dismissed").
3. **Medium. Decision note comes after the buttons.** app.js:122-128 / app.js:~466: DOM order is Approve, Dismiss, Open campaign, then the note field, so a keyboard or SR user approves before reaching "Add a note for the record". Also the reason menu (agent-ui.js:~398, 457) renders below "Open campaign", so Tab from Dismiss skips it. Fix: render the note above the footer (or inside the card before actions) and render the reason group directly after the Dismiss button, moving focus to its first button; keep `aria-controls`.
4. **Medium. Light muted text under 4.5:1.** `--ink-3` #65717C: 4.47 on `--ground` (axe: 85 nodes, e.g. `.hm-date`, `.cs-count`, `.an-asof`, `.cn-connect-line`, `.cm-col-empty`), 4.18 on accent-soft, 4.11 on agent-soft. `--good` #2E7D4F on `--good-soft`: 4.31 (`.k-pill.k-tone--good`, audiences and content). Fix in tokens.css: `--ink-3: #5B6772` (5.2 ground, 4.8 accent-soft/agent-soft), `--good: #276C46` (5.4 on good-soft). Everything else passes: ink 14-16, ink-2 8-9, accent 6.2-6.9, agent 5.4-6.0, warn 4.9+, crit 5.2+, on-accent/on-agent 6.9/6.0; dark theme all >= 4.76.
5. **Medium. Input borders fail 1.4.11.** `.k-input/.k-select` border `--line-strong` #C9C4BA is 1.74:1 on surface (css/app.css:339); chips, segmented options, cards similar (`--line` 1.33). Fix: a `--control-border: #857F74` (3.97 on surface, 3.55 on ground) for inputs, selects, checkboxes and chips; keep `--line` for decorative dividers. Dark `--line-strong` is 1.79, same fix (~#6B7885).
6. **Medium. Chart markers 22x22 and a nested-interactive error.** `g.hm-node` and `g.ch-agent-marker` are 22px (home.css hit circle r=11; 2.5.8 needs 24px or spacing, and dense days overlap; 15-22 markers per chart at 390). Raise hit r to 12-13. axe `nested-interactive` (serious, analytics `svg[viewBox="0 0 1110 260"]` and the 390 variant): the chart svg carries a role that forbids focusable children (charts.js:~574); use `role="group"` like home.js:458 does.
7. **Medium. Async states are not live.** "Writing variants…" (content.js:914) and "Thinking…" (console.js:263, 734) are injected into no pre-existing live region, and generated results/draft plan arrive silently; planner Draft shows nothing for 3s with AI off. Fix: one mounted `role="status"` per view, text updated to "Writing variants…" then "3 variants ready"; add `aria-busy` on the results container. Toast host itself is correct (polite region, 3 max, dismiss button) but is not used for approvals.
8. **Medium. Focus lost on in-view mode changes.** "Plan a campaign" removes the clicked button and focus goes to BODY (h1 `New campaign plan` is not focused; route changes do focus h1). Fix: focus the new view h1 (tabIndex -1) after mode switch. Same after Discard/Back.
9. **Low.** Content library is 52 tab stops (`.cs-lib-row`) before the composer: roving tabindex or a skip link. Chip groups (`#brief-ch-*`) lack a group label; no `aria-required`; proposal error text (agent-ui.js:~461) lacks `role="alert"`; `section[aria-labelledby=cmp-col-*]` has role listitem (axe); audiences.js:138 fallback drawer has no trap.

## Quick wins
- `--ink-3` to #5B6772 and `--good` to #276C46 (tokens.css, both light blocks only).
- `--control-border` token for inputs/selects/chips.
- Hoist `LinkBtn` out of Panel (connect.js).
- After approve/dismiss: focus card heading plus announce via status region.
- Chart marker hit radius 12; analytics svg `role="group"`.
- Move decision note above buttons; reason menu right after Dismiss.
